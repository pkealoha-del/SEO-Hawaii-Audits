What exactly is a citation?

Any online mention of your business name, address, phone number, and website — directories, maps, review sites, industry platforms. When those all agree, search engines trust you. When they don't, you slide down the map.

Will this guarantee I rank #1 on Google?

No — and anyone promising that isn't being straight with you. Citation cleanup strengthens your local foundation. It's one important piece of a bigger local SEO picture, and it's usually the cheapest piece to fix.
